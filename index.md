Privacy Policy for VALUE

Effective date: November 19, 2026

VALUE ("the app") is a timer and stopwatch app for Android, developed by Dev Vardhan.

Summary
VALUE does not collect, share or sell any personal information. The app works entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection.

Information we do not collect
We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not request the internet permission.

Data stored on your device
To work properly, the app saves some information locally on your phone: your chosen styles, your chosen alarm sound, vibration and sound options, the last timer duration you used, and the state of a running timer or stopwatch, including laps, so it can continue after the app is closed. This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Alarm sounds you add
If you add your own sound, the app uses Android's file picker and remembers the location of the file you selected so it can play it as your alarm. The file is not copied, uploaded or shared, and the app cannot access any file you did not select.

Permissions
- Notifications: to show a running timer or stopwatch and to alert you when a timer ends.
- Vibration: for alarm vibration and touch feedback.
- Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the timer and stopwatch accurate while the app is in the background or the screen is off.
- Exact alarms: so that your timer ends at the right time.
None of these permissions is used to collect data.

Children
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
